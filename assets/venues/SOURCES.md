# Venue marks

These local assets are shown only as identifiers in the Auralis venue map; the page does not imply an affiliation.

- `binance.svg`, `okx.svg`: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), recoloured white for the dark venue strip. The OKX asset uses the X-mark region of its wordmark, preserving the five-square symbol.
- `coinbase.svg`: the 48 × 48 `logoMarkData` path from [Coinbase Design System](https://github.com/coinbase/cds/blob/master/packages/common/src/hooks/useLogo.ts), in white.
- `tmgm.png`: the icon portion of [TMGM's official site logo](https://tmgm-cdn2.com/image/trading/top-logo.png), cropped and rendered white with transparency.
- `cme-group.png`: the globe portion of the [CME Group secondary logo guide image](https://brand.cmegroup.com/images/cme-group-logo_secondary.png), cropped from its white reverse variant with transparency. [CME Group's brand guide](https://brand.cmegroup.com/logo.html) allows the reverse one-colour logo on dark backgrounds.
