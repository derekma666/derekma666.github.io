Three connected research branches

One mathematical agenda, from uncertain evidence to risk-aware design.

Across probability, stochastic processes, inverse problems, scientific machine learning, and decision analysis, the programme asks one question: how can mathematical structure turn incomplete evidence into reliable action?

01

Stochastic systems

X(s,t,ω)

AI-informed random fields & risk assessment

Learning spatial and temporal variability from sparse observations, simulations, and prior knowledge—then carrying that uncertainty through to system-level risk.

  • Multivariate random fields
  • Bayesian learning & copulas
  • Uncertainty propagation
02

Visual intelligence

p(z | I, 𝓜)

Visual AI & computer vision for geomaterials

Extracting risk-relevant structure from images and physical processes, linking perception models with mechanics for explainable hazard intelligence.

  • Segmentation & representation
  • Physics-informed AI
  • Catastrophe & geohazard risk
03

Decision intelligence

  • AI-assisted decisions

Selected projects

Research across disciplines and decisions.

A selection of research projects and contributions listed on my University of Warwick profile.

Warwick IAS · Interdisciplinary Research Development Award (2026)

Integrated Risk Modelling for Decentralised Finance

Co-Investigator · With Dr Kaihua Qin (PI) and Prof Jiahua Xu (UCL)

Integrates computer security, stochastic modelling and financial economics to assess systemic risk and cascading failures in decentralised finance.

Fudan–Warwick Joint Seed Fund

AI-driven Deep Learning Prediction for Subsurface Characterisation Using Random Fields

Co-Investigator · With Dr Feng Mao (Warwick) and Prof Jian Pu (Fudan)

Combines deep learning and stochastic random fields to address spatial uncertainty in subsurface characterisation.

Shanghai Jiao Tong University–Warwick Joint Seed Fund

Next Level Risk Assessment of Landslides using Machine Learning-Based Reliability Modelling

In collaboration with Prof Lulu Zhang (Shanghai Jiao Tong University)

Combines machine learning and probabilistic reliability analysis to improve landslide risk assessment under geological and geotechnical uncertainty.

European Commission Horizon 2020 · MSCA-RISE (2020–2026)

RECYCLE — Removal and Mitigation of Pollution from the Use of Pesticides: Prevention, Recycling and Resource Management

Participating Researcher

Contributed stochastic modelling, spatial heterogeneity and uncertainty quantification to interdisciplinary environmental and subsurface research.

Warwick IAS · Early Career Fellowship (2021–2022)

Early Career Fellow in Interdisciplinary Research

Fellowship Holder

Welsh Government · Coal Tip Safety Taskforce (2022)

Coal Tip Landslip Modelling and Risk Assessment

Researcher and Policy Advisor
