WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

This notice covers the Auralis Tech research preview and this page, maintained by Dr Derek G. Ma. The main SRI Lab website has separate analytics and is outside this notice.

Page delivery

GitHub Pages serves these static pages, and Google Fonts supplies the typeface. Your browser makes requests to those providers, which may process technical request data under their own policies. These preview pages do not include an on-site analytics script or advertising cookies.

Language choice

If you change language, the site saves the choice as “sri-language” in your browser’s local storage and adds a language code to the page URL. You can remove the saved choice by clearing site data in your browser.

Email enquiries

Contact and Apply links open your email application; there is no application form on this site. If you choose to email, your message and any attachments are handled by your email provider and the recipient’s email service. Please avoid sending confidential account or trading details.

Questions

For questions about this preview or an email you have sent, write to Derek.Ma.1@warwick.ac.uk.
